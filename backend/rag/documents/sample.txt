Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of Artificial Intelligence. It allows computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every task.

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.

Artificial Intelligence is used in healthcare, education, agriculture, transportation, cybersecurity, and many other fields.
